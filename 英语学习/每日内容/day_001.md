## 💬 场景句型

| # | 英文表达 | 中文 | 例句 |
|---|---------|------|------|
| 1 | Hi, I'd like to check in for flight 307 to San Francisco. | 你好，我想办理去旧金山的307航班的登机手续。 | Hi, I'd like to check in for flight 307 to San Francisco. (直接表明来意，简洁明了) |
| 2 | Here you go. | 给你/给您。 | Can I see your passport? — Here you go. (递东西时超常用的自然表达，比 'Here is it' 自然多了) |
| 3 | Are you checking any bags today? | 您今天有托运的行李吗？ | Are you checking any bags today? — Yes, just this one suitcase. (机场必问问题，any checking bags = 托运行李) |
| 4 | Do you prefer a window or an aisle seat? | 您想要靠窗还是靠过道的座位？ | Do you prefer a window or an aisle seat? — Aisle, please. I like to stretch my legs. (座位偏好的标准问法) |
| 5 | It's a bit overweight. There's a $75 fee. | 稍微超重了，要收75美元费用。 | Your bag is 23 kilos... oh, it's a bit overweight. There's a $75 fee. (超重行李费的口语表达方式) |
| 6 | You're all set. Have a great flight! | 手续办完了，祝您旅途愉快！ | Here's your boarding pass. You're all set. Have a great flight! (值机结束的标准告别语) |
| 7 | Is that okay if I bring this as a carry-on? | 这个我可以带上飞机吗？ | I have a guitar case here. Is that okay if I bring this as a carry-on? (随身行李确认的实用问法) |
