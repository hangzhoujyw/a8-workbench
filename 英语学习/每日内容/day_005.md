## 💬 场景句型

| # | 英文表达 | 中文 | 例句 |
|---|---------|------|------|
| 1 | Excuse me, do you know how to get to the subway station? | 打扰一下，你知道怎么去地铁站吗？ | Hey, sorry to bother you. Do you know how to get to the subway station from here? |
| 2 | Is it within walking distance? | 走路能到吗？ | Thanks for the directions! Oh, one more thing — is the hotel within walking distance? |
| 3 | Can you show me on the map? | 你能在地图上给我指一下吗？ | I keep getting confused. Can you show me on the map where exactly I need to turn? |
| 4 | It's on the corner of Main Street and Fifth Avenue. | 它在主街和第五大道的拐角处。 | The coffee shop you're looking for — it's on the corner of Main Street and Fifth Avenue, right next to the bank. |
| 5 | Go straight for about two blocks, then turn left. | 直走大概两个街区，然后左转。 | So from here, go straight for about two blocks, then turn left at the traffic light. |
| 6 | You can't miss it — it's the big red building. | 你肯定能找到——就是那个大红楼。 | Trust me, you can't miss it. It's the big red building with a Starbucks on the ground floor. |
| 7 | Is there a shortcut? | 有近道吗？ | We're kind of in a rush. Is there a shortcut to the convention center? |

## 🗣️ 口语练习

**练习类型：** 跟读模仿
**跟读：问路完整对话**

先听原声2遍，然后跟读，注意模仿语调和停顿。

> 📌 Excuse me, could you tell me how to get to the train station? Sure, go straight for about three blocks, then turn right at the second traffic light. You'll see a big blue building on your left. You can't miss it.
