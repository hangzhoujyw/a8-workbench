## 💬 场景句型

| # | 英文表达 | 中文 | 例句 |
|---|---------|------|------|
| 1 | Can you take me to the train station? | 能带我去火车站吗？ | Hey, can you take me to the train station? I need to catch the 3 o'clock train. |
| 2 | How much is the fare to the airport? | 去机场多少钱？ | How much is the fare to the airport? Is it a fixed price or metered? |
| 3 | Please take the faster route if you can. | 如果可以的话，请走快的路线。 | I'm in a hurry. Please take the faster route if you can, thanks. |
| 4 | Can I pay by card? | 我可以刷卡吗？ | Do you take cards? I don't have much cash on me. Can I pay by card? |
| 5 | Here you go. Keep the change. | 给你，不用找了。 | The meter says $18.50. Here you go. Keep the change, please. |
| 6 | Can you stop here? This is fine. | 能停这儿吗？这儿就可以了。 | Oh, this is perfect. Can you stop here? This is fine, thank you. |
| 7 | Could you hold your phone up so I can see the GPS? | 能把手机举起来让我看清导航吗？ | The traffic looks bad. Could you hold your phone up so I can see the GPS? |
| 8 | We should be there in about fifteen minutes. | 大概十五分钟能到。 | We should be there in about fifteen minutes, depending on traffic. |

## 🗣️ 口语练习

**练习类型：** 跟读模仿
**Taxi Dialogue Shadow Practice**

跟读一段简短的真实打车对话，模仿语音语调和节奏，练习完整打车流程的英语表达。

> 📌 Passenger: Taxi! Excuse me, are you available? Driver: Yes, where to? Passenger: Please take me to the train station. Driver: Sure, hop in. Passenger: How long will it take? Driver: About fifteen minutes, depending on traffic.
