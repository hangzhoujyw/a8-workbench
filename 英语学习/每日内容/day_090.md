## 💬 场景句型

| # | 英文表达 | 中文 | 例句 |
|---|---------|------|------|
| 1 | Let me walk you through how this works. | 我来带你一步步了解它是怎么运作的。 | Before we dive into the numbers, let me walk you through how this product actually works in practice. |
| 2 | The key differentiator here is... | 这里的关键差异化因素是…… | The key differentiator here is our proprietary algorithm that cuts processing time by 40%. |
| 3 | I'll hand it over to you for a quick demo. | 接下来交给你来做个小演示。 | Our tech lead will hand it over to you for a quick demo of the actual interface. |
| 4 | Does that make sense so far? | 到目前为止清楚吗？ | We've covered the cost savings — does that make sense so far, or should I clarify anything? |
| 5 | Let me put this into perspective for you. | 我帮你把这件事放到具体场景里来看。 | A 15-minute setup sounds long, but let me put this into perspective for you — it used to take three hours. |
| 6 | This is where things get really interesting. | 这才是真正厉害的地方。 | We've shown you the basics, but this is where things get really interesting — the automation features. |
| 7 | Feel free to jump in if you have questions. | 有问题随时可以打断我。 | I'll be going through three main features, but feel free to jump in if you have questions. |

## 🗣️ 口语练习

**练习类型：** 自言自语
**模拟产品推介 presentation（60-90秒版）**

想象你正在向一位潜在客户做产品介绍。用英语完整地说一段 60-90 秒的产品推介，内容包含：产品名称、一句话价值主张、一个核心优势、以及一个具体使用场景。

> 📌 练习时用手机录音或录像。完成后回答以下问题自我评估：
1. 你的语速是否适中，有没有因为紧张越说越快？
2. 是否有意识地用了至少两处停顿来强调重点？
3. 核心优势有没有给出具体数字或实例支撑？

参考框架：
'So today I want to share [product name] with you. It's essentially [one-line value prop]. The biggest advantage is [key differentiator]. For example, [specific use case].'

尝试脱离这个框架，用自己的语言自然地说出来。
