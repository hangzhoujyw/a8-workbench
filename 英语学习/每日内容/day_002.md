## 💬 场景句型

| # | 英文表达 | 中文 | 例句 |
|---|---------|------|------|
| 1 | Do you have anything to declare? | 您有什么需要申报的吗？ | The officer asked me, 'Do you have anything to declare?' I said no because I only had my clothes and laptop. |
| 2 | I have nothing to declare. | 我没有什么需要申报的。 | I walked through the green channel and said, 'I have nothing to declare,' but they still asked me to open my bag. |
| 3 | I'm here for tourism / business. | 我来这里旅游/出差。 | When he asked the purpose of my visit, I said, 'I'm here for tourism, just for a week.' |
| 4 | Here's my passport and declaration form. | 这是我的护照和申报表。 | I had everything ready before I reached the counter. 'Here's my passport and declaration form,' I said. |
| 5 | How long will you be staying? | 您打算停留多久？ | The officer looked at my return ticket and asked, 'How long will you be staying?' I said about ten days. |
| 6 | Where will you be staying? | 您会住在哪里？ | When they asked where I'd be staying, I showed them the hotel confirmation on my phone. |
| 7 | You can go through the green channel. | 您可以走绿色通道。 | After checking my form, the officer stamped my passport and said, 'You're all set, go through the green channel.' |
| 8 | Do I need to fill out a customs form? | 我需要填写海关申报表吗？ | I wasn't sure if I needed a form, so I asked, 'Do I need to fill out a customs form?' They said yes for international flights. |

## 🗣️ 口语练习

**练习类型：** 跟读模仿
**海关对话跟读练习**

先听ESL Podcast原文3遍，熟悉节奏和语调后，进行影子跟读。重点模仿句子的重音和停顿位置。

> 📌 Officer: Do you have your customs form?
You: Yes, I filled it out on the plane.
Officer: May I see your passport? What's the purpose of your visit?
You: I'm here for tourism. Just a short vacation.
Officer: How long will you be staying?
You: About ten days.
Officer: Do you have anything to declare?
You: No, nothing to declare.
