## 💬 场景句型

| # | 英文表达 | 中文 | 例句 |
|---|---------|------|------|
| 1 | Excuse me, do you know where the exit is? | 打扰一下，你知道出口在哪吗？ | Excuse me, do you know where the exit is? I need to get some fresh air. |
| 2 | Can you tell me how to get to the gate? | 你能告诉我怎么去登机口吗？ | My gate is B12. Can you tell me how to get there from here? |
| 3 | Is it within walking distance? | 走路能到吗？ | Is the shuttle bus stop within walking distance, or should I take a taxi? |
| 4 | Go straight and turn left at the corner. | 直走，然后在拐角左转。 | Go straight and turn left at the corner. You can't miss it. |
| 5 | It's on your right. | 在你右手边。 | Keep walking, it's on your right, next to the coffee shop. |
| 6 | You can't miss it. | 你肯定能找到（很明显）。 | The kiosk has a big blue sign—you can't miss it. |
| 7 | Can you show me on the map? | 你能在地图上给我指一下吗？ | I'm totally lost. Can you show me on the map where that is? |
| 8 | Thanks a bunch! | 太感谢了！（口语化表达） | Thanks a bunch! You're a lifesaver. |

## 🗣️ 口语练习

**练习类型：** 跟读模仿
**跟读练习：问路基本句**

先仔细听对话原声，然后用相同的节奏和语调跟读句子，模仿母语者的连读和重音。
